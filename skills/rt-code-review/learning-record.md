# Learning records

A **learning record** notes one thing the developer has shown they know about a repo, so the lesson writer builds on it and never repeats it. Write each to `<learning workspace>/learning-records/NNNN-<dash-case-name>.md` with the next free number, only on evidence in this conversation:

| The developer | Record |
|---|---|
| Pastes a full-score result, such as `L0003: 4/4` | They can do that lesson's win. |
| Pastes misses, such as `L0003: 2/4, missed Q1, Q3` | Nothing yet. Explain each missed question; record the corrected misconception once their answer shows they understand. |
| Says they already know something | That prior knowledge, and how deep they say it goes. |
| Says what they want from this repo | Update `MISSION.md` once they confirm, and record the change. |

A lesson shown is not a lesson learned. Skip terms the repo's `GLOSSARY.md` defines. Shape:

````
# <what was learned>

<1 to 3 sentences: what they know now, and why it matters for the next lesson.>

- Lesson: <L number, or none>
- Evidence: <the result line, or what they said>
````

When a later record contradicts an earlier one, add `Status: superseded by <file>` under the earlier title and leave the rest.
